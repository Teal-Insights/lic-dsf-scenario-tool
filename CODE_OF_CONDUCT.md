# Working together

We welcome contributors and users with different backgrounds and levels of technical experience. Focus criticism on the work, explain disagreements constructively, and respect other people's time and privacy.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others, publish personal information without consent, or post illegal material. Do not submit confidential workbooks, credentials, exploit details or private analytical records to public channels. These expectations apply to project-controlled issues, pull requests and support discussions, including interactions with younger participants.

Teal Insights maintains these channels. Report a conduct concern privately to [lte@tealinsights.com](mailto:lte@tealinsights.com?subject=LIC-DSF%20conduct%20concern). Describe the concern and where it occurred, sharing only the information needed to assess it. Do not repost abusive, illegal or sensitive material publicly to draw attention to it. Reports are handled on a best-effort basis without a guaranteed response time. We cannot promise absolute confidentiality where responding to a serious safety issue or legal obligation requires disclosure.

Maintainers may request a correction, remove inappropriate material from channels they control, close a discussion, restrict participation or block an account. They will consider context and severity. This does not create authority to access or moderate users' private local workspaces. A hosted content or collaboration feature would need a separate operating policy.

For a software vulnerability, use the [security reporting route](SECURITY.md). For a product improvement, start with the user problem and consult [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).
