# Help shape the tool


This is an early-stage release. We are developing the tool iteratively and welcome co-design with ministries of finance, international financial institutions and other stakeholders. Your experience can help us understand what works, what is confusing and what would make the tool more useful.

## Send feedback

[Email lte@tealinsights.com](mailto:lte@tealinsights.com?subject=LIC-DSF%20Scenario%20Tool%20preview%20feedback).

Please include whatever is useful from this short list:

- What were you trying to do?
- What happened, and what did you expect?
- Which app version and operating system were you using?
- What would make this easier or more useful in your work?
- If you are comfortable sharing it, what is your role or institution, and may we follow up?

You do not need to answer every question. A short description is welcome. Opening the email link does not attach a workbook or send application data automatically. You choose what to send.

Please leave confidential workbooks, analytical data, private notes, credentials and unreviewed logs out of feedback. A non-sensitive example or a description is usually enough to start a conversation.

## What happens next

Teal Insights reviews feedback on a best-effort basis. We may ask follow-up questions or invite you to discuss a workflow. There is no guaranteed response time or promise to implement a suggestion. Maintainers set priorities based on usefulness, evidence, scope, maintenance and safety.

Feedback is correspondence, not anonymous telemetry. Email addresses and any role or institution you choose to provide are contact information, not proof of identity or affiliation. Do not send information you do not want to share by email.

## Other ways to help

Documentation corrections, reproducible bug reports and focused code contributions are welcome under our contribution guidelines. AI-assisted contributions must meet the same standards of understanding, relevance and validation as other submissions.

For a suspected security vulnerability, use the project's private security-reporting route once verified for this release. Do not publish exploit details or confidential material in an issue or pull request.

The tool is not an official IMF or World Bank product. An invitation to co-design does not imply institutional endorsement.
